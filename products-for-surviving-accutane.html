Products for Surviving Accutane

By Money Well Spent • Beauty & Skincare

Accutane can be challenging, especially when it comes to dry skin and lips. These are products that helped me, plus a few options I researched for people looking for extra sun protection.

1. Vanicream Moisturizing Cream

When your skin is extra dry, a simple moisturizer can make a big difference. Vanicream Moisturizing Cream is a good option to consider for dry skin. It has a rich texture, so it can feel heavier than a lightweight face lotion.

Pros
  • Rich, moisturizing cream
  • Useful when skin feels very dry
  • Simple option to keep in your skincare routine
Cons
  • Thicker texture may feel heavy for some people
  • Everyone's skin reacts differently

2. Tarte Maracuja Juicy Lip Gloss

Dry lips can be one of the most frustrating parts of Accutane. I really like Tarte's Maracuja Juicy lip products because they feel hydrating and give your lips some color. There are several versions, including regular glosses, lightly plumping options, shimmer shades, and a pH-shifting version. The plumping version has a minty feeling and gives a subtle plumping effect.

If you want to wear color but find regular lipstick or gloss drying, these are worth looking into. One downside is that each version does not always come in the exact same shades. I've tried the different varieties except for the pH-shifting gloss.

Pros
  • Hydrating feel
  • Multiple styles and finishes
  • Color options for different preferences
  • Plumping version gives a gentle minty feeling
Cons
  • Same shades may not be available in every finish
  • May need reapplying after eating or drinking

3. Too Faced Pillow Lip Mask

A lip mask can be helpful when your lips feel dry and need extra moisture. The Too Faced Pillow Lip Mask is another option to consider. Keep in mind that the shades can look stronger than you might expect, and the color may not apply evenly on everyone.

Pros
  • Made for lips that need extra care
  • Can add color as well as moisture
Cons
  • Some shades may be more intense than expected
  • Color may look uneven depending on your lips

5. COOLA Spray-On Sunscreen

Sun protection is important, and a spray can make reapplying sunscreen more convenient when you're wearing makeup. COOLA's spray-on sunscreen is one option. Make sure you follow the label directions and apply enough product for the protection listed.

Pros
  • Convenient spray format
  • Can be useful for reapplication
  • Designed for sun protection
Cons
  • Sprays can be difficult to apply evenly
  • Follow the product label; don't rely on a light mist alone

7. ScarAway Silicone Scar Sheets

I used ScarAway silicone scar sheets to help with the appearance of marks. The sheets are reusable, although one came loose while I was sleeping. Over time, my marks became less noticeable. Natural healing also plays a part, so I can't say the sheets alone caused the change.

I've gotten to a point where I can hardly see my scars anymore. I know that natural healing along with these sheets sped up the process.

Final Thoughts

Accutane can be a challenging experience, and dryness can make everyday things like wearing lip products or spending time outside more difficult. These are products and options that may help, but everyone's skin and experience are different. Follow your prescriber's instructions and ask your dermatologist about symptoms or products you're unsure about.
